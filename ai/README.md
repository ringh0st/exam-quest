# AI

Prompts, evaluation sets and reports for the AI pipeline.

- `prompts/`: versioned prompt files (reading pages, writing lessons, checking answers, moderation)
- `eval/`: test material (anonymised photos and texts) and expected results
- `reports/`: accuracy and cost per page, per model

The first goal: read real Finnish textbook pages and handwriting with at least 95% word accuracy,
and keep the cost of a 3-page scan at or below about $0.20.
